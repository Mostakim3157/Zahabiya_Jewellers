Our Story · قصتنا

Three generations of gold in the heart of Souq Waqif

From a single counter in the old Gold Souq to Qatar's online gold destination — every piece still finished by hand.

Where pearl-diving heritage meets modern craft

Our founder began trading gold in the lanes of Souq Waqif, serving families for weddings, Eid and every milestone in between. Today our atelier pairs traditional Gulf motifs — Sadu weaving, the crescent, the palm — with precise modern finishing.
