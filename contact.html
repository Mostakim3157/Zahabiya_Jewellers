Frequently asked

How is the price of my jewellery calculated?

Price = gold weight × today's rate for its karat, plus a making charge shown separately. Rates update through the day on our Gold Rates page.

Can I exchange my old gold?

Yes. Bring any hallmarked gold to the showroom; we test and weigh it in front of you and credit it at the live rate toward a new piece.

Do you deliver outside Doha?

We deliver insured across Qatar — same day in Doha for orders before 2 pm, 1–2 days elsewhere.

Can I resize or engrave a ring?

Resizing is free within 30 days. Engraving in English or Arabic is free on wedding bands.

What payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, NAPS debit, cash on delivery up to QAR 10,000, and instalments with partner banks.
